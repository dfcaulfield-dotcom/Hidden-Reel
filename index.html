        How to play
        
    
        Five films and the people who made them, with every name removed. Work out all of it.
        
    
          
      
        1. Lines are credits. A gold line means that person directed that film. A pale line means they acted in it (among the ten top-billed). No line means no credit at all.
          2. 
      
        2. You get the shapes. Each film shows its year and the letter count of its title. Each person shows a birth year and the letter count of their name.
          3. 
      
        3. Find your own way in. Nothing is locked and nothing tells you where to begin. The tile with the most lines is the most pinned down, so it's usually the place to start.
          4. 
      
        4. Find the missing name. One more actor or director is credited on all five films but isn't shown. Guess it whenever you think you know.
          5. 
    
        
    
        Every puzzle has exactly one solution. There's no clock, so take your time; pen and paper help.
        
    
        You start with 100 points. A wrong guess costs 2, a hint costs 5, and revealing an answer costs 10. Solve everything unaided and you keep all 100.
