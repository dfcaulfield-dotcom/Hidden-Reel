    What links all six films?

    How to play

    1. Six blurred frames. Each comes with a clue. Pick the film it describes. You get two tries: 10 points first time, 5 on the second.
    2. Every film is a clue. Right or wrong, the frame comes into focus and the title is revealed.
    3. Find the hidden link. All six films share something. You get one guess, and it's worth more the earlier you make it: 40 points after two films, down to 16 after all six.

    You don't need to have seen them all. A new puzzle arrives every day at midnight.
